# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing

### Role Summary
QA and testing specialists ensure product quality, validate acceptance criteria, and identify bugs or edge cases before release. They collaborate with developers and product teams to maintain quality standards throughout the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute manual and automated tests across features
- Document defects and track resolution
- Collaborate with developers on testability and test coverage
- Verify fixes and validate resolution of reported issues
- Support release validation and smoke testing
- Identify and escalate quality risks

### Goals
- Ensure quality standards are met before release
- Reduce defects in production
- Provide confidence in releases through comprehensive testing
- Maintain clear test documentation and traceability

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and test case documentation
- Defect logs and issue reporting
- QA sign-off in pull request reviews
- Release readiness assessments
- Collaboration with developers on edge cases and testing strategies

---

## Product Leads

### Role Summary
Product Leads provide strategic direction on product vision, prioritization, and cross-team dependencies. They align engineering with business goals and guide product managers on long-term strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define product strategy and vision
- Guide product managers on prioritization and roadmap alignment
- Resolve cross-team conflicts on priorities and dependencies
- Escalate risks and dependencies that impact product delivery
- Measure product outcomes and iterate based on data
- Represent product interests in sponsor-level discussions

### Goals
- Maximize product impact and customer satisfaction
- Ensure alignment between engineering, product, and business teams
- Drive iterative improvements based on metrics and feedback
- Reduce organizational friction through clear prioritization

### Typical Communication
- Strategic roadmap updates and vision communication
- Escalation reviews with Project Managers and sponsors
- Stakeholder briefings on product direction
- Retrospective participation and improvement reviews
- Cross-team dependency coordination

---

## Stakeholders / Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholders represent business interests, organizational priorities, and funding. Sponsors approve project initiation, allocate resources, and remove high-level blockers that teams cannot resolve independently.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context, constraints, and strategic alignment
- Approve project initiation and resource allocation
- Escalate and resolve sponsor-level blockers
- Participate in key milestone reviews and decision gates
- Communicate organizational priorities to project teams
- Support risk escalation paths when needed

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Manage organizational priorities and trade-offs
- Maintain alignment between projects and strategy
- Reduce cycle time by unblocking high-impact issues

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and decision gate approvals
- Executive status reports (monthly or milestone-based)
- Escalation resolution and blocker removal
- Strategic alignment discussions
- Resource and funding decisions

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide architecture decisions, mentor developers, and ensure technical quality and scalability. They bridge engineering and product by providing technical perspective on feasibility and trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design and architecture decisions
- Review designs for scalability, maintainability, and security
- Mentor developers on technical best practices
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and debt
- Support estimation and planning with technical insights
- Participate in code reviews for architectural compliance

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and secure solutions
- Reduce technical debt while maintaining velocity
- Build team capability through mentoring
- Ensure long-term sustainability of the codebase

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and decision records
- Architecture guidance in kickoff and planning meetings
- Code review comments and architectural feedback
- Technical mentoring and guidance in standups
- Risk escalation on technical challenges

---

## Support / Customer Success

### Role Summary
Support teams represent customer needs and feedback, ensuring product decisions account for customer impact and usability. They provide frontline insights into how customers use the product and where pain points exist.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and summarize customer feedback and pain points
- Identify support gaps and usability issues
- Participate in feature validation and acceptance testing
- Support release communication and customer documentation
- Escalate critical customer-impacting issues
- Advocate for customer perspective in prioritization discussions

### Goals
- Maximize customer satisfaction and product usability
- Reduce support burden through proactive issue resolution
- Identify feature improvements and gaps from customer perspective
- Ensure releases communicate value clearly to customers

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and trend reports
- Usability feedback on features under development
- Release communication and customer-facing documentation
- Feature requests and customer pain points in backlog discussions
- Escalation of critical customer-impacting issues
